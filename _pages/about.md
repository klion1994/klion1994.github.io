I am currently a lecturer at the School of Computer Science, Inner Mongolia University. My main research interests include smart sensing, mobile computing, indoor localization and navigation.

Previously, I obtained my B.E. degree in 2017, M.S. degree in 2019, and Ph.D. in 2024, all from Inner Mongolia University, under the supervision of Prof. [Baoqi Huang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=zh-CN&user=niymf28AAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate). During my Ph.D. studies, I received several scholarships, including the National Scholarship in 2020 and the Academic Scholarship of Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region in 2021.
